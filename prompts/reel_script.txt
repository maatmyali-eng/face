SEGMENT 1 — PATTERN INTERRUPT HOOK (0-3 seconds)

  Spoken duration: Must be speakable in exactly 2-3 seconds at normal pace.
  Pacing note: Write for TTS delivery — short punchy sentences, no commas.

SEGMENT 2 — SETUP AND PROOF (3-15 seconds)
  Purpose: Justify the hook. Deliver early value. Keep the viewer watching.
  Word count: 60-80 words.
  Spoken duration: 12 seconds at average TTS pace (150 words per minute).
  Structure: Confirm the hook claim → introduce the subject → first revelation.
  RETENTION HOOK at the 15-second mark: End this segment with a cliffhanger or

  Pacing note: Use sentence breaks for TTS pauses. No sentences longer than 15 words.

  Purpose: Deliver the core content. Earn the follow/share.
  Word count: 100-130 words.
  Spoken duration: 30 seconds at average TTS pace.

    - Use concrete numbers and specifics (not "cheap" — say "under 500 taka")
    - For educational content: use "Here's what most people don't know..." between points
    - For inspirational content: build emotional peaks — the third point should be the most powerful
  Language note for Bilingual: This segment can mix — key insights in Bangla feel more personal.

SEGMENT 4 — CTA CLOSE (45-60 seconds)
  Purpose: Drive the action. Make following feel like the obvious next step.
  Word count: MAXIMUM 20 words.
  Spoken duration: 8 seconds.
  CTA formula: [Action verb] + [what they get] + [when/why now].
  Examples:
    - "Follow this page. We drop hidden gems like this every single day."
    - "Save this video. You'll thank yourself when you actually make this trip."
    - "Tag your travel partner below. This trip is better with someone else."
    - "Comment your city. I'll tell you the hidden gem closest to you."
